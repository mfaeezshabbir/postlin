## 🚀 Excited to Share: Postlin - Your AI-Powered LinkedIn Content Assistant

I'm thrilled to announce the completion of **Postlin**, a full-stack application that revolutionizes how professionals manage their LinkedIn presence!

### 💡 What is Postlin?

Postlin is an intelligent content management platform that helps you:

✨ **AI-Powered Content Generation**
- Create engaging LinkedIn posts using Google Gemini AI
- Generate AI-powered images to complement your content
- Smart drafting system for refining your ideas

📅 **Smart Scheduling**
- Schedule posts for optimal engagement times
- Automated publishing with background workers
- Never miss your posting window again

📊 **Comprehensive Management**
- Draft management with version control
- Publishing history and analytics
- Seamless LinkedIn OAuth integration

### 🛠️ Tech Stack

Built with modern, production-ready technologies:
- **Frontend:** Next.js 15 (App Router), React, TailwindCSS
- **Backend:** Next.js API Routes, Prisma ORM
- **Database:** MongoDB Atlas
- **AI Integration:** Google Gemini API
- **Authentication:** NextAuth.js with LinkedIn OAuth
- **Deployment:** Vercel-ready

### 🎯 Key Features

1️⃣ **Intelligent Drafting System**
   - Save and manage multiple drafts
   - AI-assisted content improvement
   - Real-time preview

2️⃣ **Automated Scheduling**
   - Dynamic scheduling algorithm
   - Background job processing
   - Retry mechanism for reliability

3️⃣ **Seamless Publishing**
   - One-click LinkedIn publishing
   - Base64 image optimization
   - Error handling and recovery

### 🌟 Why I Built This

As someone passionate about content creation and technology, I wanted to solve a common problem: **consistency in LinkedIn engagement**. Postlin automates the tedious parts while keeping you in control of your brand voice.
